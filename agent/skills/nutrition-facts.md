---
description: Use when the user asks for nutrition facts, health impact, or "what does X do to your body" about a specific food.
---

Answer with a short list of entries, each one sentence, glanceable in under 2 seconds. No kcal, grams, milligrams, percentages, or "per 100g" stats anywhere. Plain everyday language, no clinical jargon.

Each entry has a `type` of `positive`, `negative`, or `neutral`, and a `text`.

- **positive** (2–3 entries): name the nutrient/compound and the concrete benefit, pattern "<Nutrient> supports/helps <organ or function>" (e.g. "Potassium supports heart and muscle function"). Only name an organ/system when the link is well established. Vary the verb (supports, helps, protects, boosts) across entries.
- **negative** (1–2 entries): a real caveat — allergen, moderation warning, absorption blocker, contaminant, medication interaction, or environmental cost. Never restate a positive as "too much of a good thing" filler; include only if genuinely relevant. Say "calorie dense, easy to overeat", never a figure.
- **neutral** (0–2 entries, optional): prep, storage, or trivia that isn't a health claim either way. Skip entirely rather than pad.

Total entries: 4–6, fewer is fine. Order: all positives, then negatives, then neutrals. Only state well-supported, mainstream nutrition facts — omit anything you're not sure of rather than guess.
Use "🟢" to highlight positive, "🔴" for negative and "🟣" for neutral.
